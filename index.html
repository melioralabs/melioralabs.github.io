FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting a conversation with us.

What type of companies do you work with?
We work with companies of any size — from small businesses wanting to add technology to their services, to medium enterprises and large banks or fintechs needing full modernization. If it's digital, we can help. We serve clients in the US and Mexico.
How much does a project cost?
Pricing depends on the scope, complexity, and tier of your project. We believe technology should be accessible — our pricing is designed to be fair for every company size. We start with a free consultation to understand your needs and provide a custom estimate. No commitment required.
Do you work remotely?
Yes — 100% remote. We serve clients across the US and Mexico through video calls, shared tools, and async communication. No limits on where we work together.
What does go-live support include?
Go-live support is included in every project. We provide a grace period after launch to cover any errors, bugs, or unexpected issues — at no extra cost. You are never alone after delivery. The duration is agreed at the start of each project and documented in your service agreement.
What documents do I receive at the end?
Every project includes a complete documentation package: Project Guide, Cost Breakdown, Billing Agreement, Technical Specifications, and a Handover Document. Nothing is left as a black box — your team owns everything after we're done.
How long does a project take?
Timelines depend on scope. Small tools and enhancements: 2–4 weeks. Medium projects: 1–3 months. Large modernizations: 3–6+ months. We always provide a realistic estimate during the free initial consultation — before any commitment.
Do I need technical knowledge to work with you?
Not at all. We translate complex technology into simple, human language. Our diagrams and presentations are designed for decision makers, not just engineers. You bring the business knowledge — we bring the technical expertise. Together we build the right solution.
Do you work with companies in Mexico?
Yes. We have deep experience working across the US and Mexico — including regulatory, cultural, and technical differences between both markets. We are bilingual EN/ES and work comfortably across time zones.
